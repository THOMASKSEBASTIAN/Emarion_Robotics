⚡ 6 Industry-Standard Engineering Pathways

Master Tomorrow's Technologies Today

Comprehensive, hands-on learning modules engineered to take students from beginner concepts to real-world hardware, drone flight, and AI deployment.

Track 01 • All Levels

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Demystifying AI Through Practical Logic

Step beyond passive chatbots. Students master how algorithms process patterns, train computer vision models, and explore neural reasoning through visual nodes and Python syntax.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ Supervised & Unsupervised Machine Learning logic
  • ▹ Deep Learning neural network structures
  • ▹ Computer Vision & Real-time Object Recognition
  • ▹ Responsible & Ethical Generative AI tools
Hands-On Capstone: Train custom image-recognition models to identify recycled plastics and deploy a predictive AI classifier.
Track 02 • Core STEM

Robotics & Automation

Mechanical Assembly & Microcontroller Brains

Combine gear dynamics with smart microcontroller programming. Students construct multi-wheel chassis rovers, solder components, and wire tactile feedback sensors.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ Arduino microcontroller IDE & C++ fundamentals
  • ▹ Motor drivers, H-Bridges, and servo actuation
  • ▹ Ultrasonic sonar distance sensors & infrared tracking
  • ▹ Mechanical gears, torque, and chassis dynamics
Hands-On Capstone: Build and compete with fully autonomous obstacle-avoiding and line-following robotic rovers.
Track 03 • Connected Tech

IoT & Smart Systems

Connecting Physical Devices to Cloud Networks

Turn ordinary rooms and buildings into intelligent systems. Learn how sensors send environmental telemetry to online dashboards and trigger physical relays remotely.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ ESP32 & Wi-Fi/Bluetooth microcontrollers
  • ▹ MQTT protocols & RESTful cloud integration
  • ▹ Temperature, humidity, soil, and gas telemetry
  • ▹ Smart relay switches and automated notifications
Hands-On Capstone: Construct a complete Smart Home Automation prototype with mobile dashboard control and leak alerts.
Track 04 • Aerospace & Aero

Drone Technology & Aerial Robotics

Aerodynamics, Flight Controllers & Autonomy

Explore the physics of lift, drag, and thrust. Students configure electronic speed controllers (ESCs), calibrate IMU gyroscopes, and pilot micro-drones in our safety flight arena.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ Quadcopter physics: Pitch, roll, yaw, and thrust
  • ▹ Flight controller configuration and PID tuning
  • ▹ Transmitter/receiver binding and fail-safe safety rules
  • ▹ Waypoint mission programming & telemetry
Hands-On Capstone: Program autonomous flight maneuvers, navigating an indoor obstacle course via preset coordinates.
Track 05 • Digital Prototyping

3D Design, CAD & Rapid Prototyping

From Creative Spatial Ideas to Physical Prints

Master industrial CAD software. Students build spatial intuition, design custom protective enclosures for microchips, and operate high-precision 3D printers.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ Parametric Computer-Aided Design (CAD) geometry
  • ▹ Tolerances, snap-fits, and mechanical hinges
  • ▹ Slicing software, infill densities, and nozzle temperatures
  • ▹ Rapid prototype iteration and stress testing
Hands-On Capstone: Design and print a custom aerodynamic drone fuselage or robotic gripper mechanism.
Track 06 • Pure Hardware

Embedded Systems & Circuit Prototyping

Hardware-Level C/C++ & Electronic Breadboarding

For students and developers wanting deep mastery beneath high-level abstractions. Learn direct register manipulation, interrupt handlers, and custom circuit layout.

Core Topics Covered:

  • ▹ Low-level C/C++ register programming
  • ▹ Analog-to-Digital Conversion (ADC) & PWM control
  • ▹ Timers, external interrupts, and power management
  • ▹ Breadboard wiring, pull-up resistors, and PCB schematics
Hands-On Capstone: Breadboard a standalone microcontroller clock with custom LED matrix driver without external libraries.
